ScienceAutoBot

Automated science education through short videos.

About the project

ScienceAutoBot is an educational project designed to create short French-language videos about scientific facts, nature, animals and discoveries.

How it works

The project uses software tools to help prepare educational video content. The account owner reviews and remains responsible for the content published through their TikTok account.

Responsible use

ScienceAutoBot is intended for lawful educational use. It must be used in accordance with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Users should verify scientific information before relying on it.
